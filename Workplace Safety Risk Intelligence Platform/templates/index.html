Workplace Safety Risk Intelligence Platform

An AI-powered decision support system for predicting workplace accident severity and evaluating safety risk using historical industrial data.

This platform leverages deep learning models trained on real industrial accident data to estimate the expected severity of workplace accidents and the probability of severe incidents. It enables organizations to assess current safety conditions and understand how improvements in operational or safety-related factors can reduce accident risk.
